Fox Drop privacy policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

The short version

Fox Drop has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Your wishlist and settings stay on your device. The only things that leave it are what you choose to post in Fox Chat, the optional invite-only chat: your chat nickname, your messages and your event tips. Those are stored with Google Firebase so your crew can see them.

This policy covers the Fox Drop Android app (from Google Play or the download page) and Fox Drop for Windows at lateraldamage.github.io/fox-drop/app.

What stays on your device

Your wishlist, the live events you add for yourself, your alert settings, people you've hidden in chat, and a note of what the app last saw (so it knows what's new) are saved only on your phone, or in your browser for the Windows app. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

Your anonymous ID

The first time Fox Drop opens, it signs in to Google Firebase anonymously. That gives this copy of the app a random ID with no name, email or phone number attached. The ID is what lets everyone see official live events, and what lets Fox Chat know which messages are yours. Firebase, like any server, also sees your IP address when the app connects. Uninstalling the app removes the ID from your device.

Fox Chat (optional)

Fox Chat only works if you join with an invite code from the admin. If you don't join, nothing below applies to you. When you join or post, Fox Drop stores the following with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, servers in the United States):

  • the chat nickname you choose (please use a nickname, not your real name)
  • the messages you send, the event tips you share and the comments you leave, each with the time it was sent
  • reports you make about a message, which only the admin can see
  • whether the admin has muted or banned your ID

Who can see it: other people in the crew (everyone who has the current invite code) and the admin. Only the admin can see reports, the member list and the invite code. Approved tips become official events, which every Fox Drop user sees as a title, time and note, without your name.

How long it's kept: until the admin deletes it. You can delete your own tips yourself. To have your messages or your nickname removed, ask the admin in the chat or open an issue on GitHub (you don't need to say who you are in public; just ask for a way to reach the admin).

Keeping it safe: everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server-side security rules stop anyone without the invite code from reading the chat, and stop members from changing anyone else's posts. Anyone can report a message to the admin or hide everything from a person.

Fox Drop doesn't sell or share this with anyone else and doesn't use it for ads. Google stores and processes it for Fox Drop under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

What the app downloads

To show the Item Shop, news, server status and live events, the app downloads public information from these services. It sends them no personal information, only the ordinary web request that every app and browser makes, which includes your IP address.

  • fortnite-api.com for the Item Shop, news and cosmetics
  • status.epicgames.com for server status
  • Epic Games' public Fortnite content page for in-game notices and tournaments (Android app only)
  • Google Firebase for official live events and, if you join, Fox Chat (see above)
  • Google's gstatic.com, which serves the Firebase code the Windows app runs
  • lateraldamage.github.io (GitHub Pages) for the live-event list
  • The image hosts those services link to, for item pictures

Each service has its own privacy policy for the requests it receives.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to alert you about the shop, updates, news, live events and new Fox Chat messages and tips. The Windows app asks your browser for the same permission and only notifies you while it's open.
  • Alarms & reminders: so live-event reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup: to set event reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: to download the information above.

Children

Fox Drop is meant for players aged 13 and up, matching Fortnite's Teen rating, and isn't directed at children under 13. Fox Chat is invite-only and moderated by the admin, and asks for a nickname rather than a real name. If you believe a child under 13 has posted in Fox Chat, ask the admin or open an issue on GitHub and their posts will be deleted.
