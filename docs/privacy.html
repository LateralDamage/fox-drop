Fox Drop privacy policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

The short version

Fox Drop doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your wishlist, the live events you add, your alert settings and a note of what the app last saw (so it knows what's new) are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

To show the Item Shop, news, server status and live events, the app downloads public information from these services. It sends them no personal information, only the ordinary web request that every app and browser makes, which includes your IP address.

  • fortnite-api.com for the Item Shop, news and cosmetics
  • status.epicgames.com for server status
  • Epic Games' public Fortnite content page for in-game notices and tournaments
  • lateraldamage.github.io (GitHub Pages) for the live-event list
  • The image hosts those services link to, for item pictures

Each service has its own privacy policy for the requests it receives.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to alert you about the shop, updates, news and live events.
  • Alarms & reminders: so live-event reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup: to set event reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: to download the information above.

Children

Fox Drop is meant for players aged 13 and up, matching Fortnite's Teen rating. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Not affiliated with Epic Games

Fox Drop is an unofficial fan app. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Epic Games. Fortnite is a trademark of Epic Games, Inc.

Questions and changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
